How the queue is calculated

Urgency (10/20/30) + essential medical or shelter need (10) + access barrier (6 each, maximum 12) + waiting time (up to 10). Scores support review; they never automatically dispatch help. Race, income, and ZIP code are not used.

DESIGN NOTES

Built for clarity and access

The demo connects a household plan, a concise need report, and a transparent coordinator queue.

Faster

One short form produces a structured request. Offline browser storage lets the demonstration work without an account or server.

More accurate

Each request has an explicit urgency, category, location, and visible priority factors. A person makes the final call.

More equitable

People can indicate transport, communication, or essential equipment barriers. The queue gives those needs visibility without using race or income as a proxy.

What a real pilot needs

Official alert feeds, secure identity and consent, encrypted sync, multilingual review, accessibility testing, and trained emergency-management partners.

This is a simulation for education. It is not an official warning system or dispatch tool.
